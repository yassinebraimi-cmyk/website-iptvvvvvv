TERMS OF SERVICE

1. Service Availability & Uptime SLA

Aetheria IPTV guarantees a 99.99% network uptime SLA on our global edge clusters. Automated failover reroutes stream traffic instantaneously should an individual node undergo maintenance.

2. Simultaneous Connection Allowance

Subscribers must adhere to the screen limit specified in their active plan (1, 2, or 3 concurrent streams). Exceeding simultaneous stream limits may trigger automated line locking.

3. Automated Credential Setup

Credentials are provisioned automatically upon checkout. Subscribers are responsible for keeping M3U URLs and Xtream API passwords secure.
